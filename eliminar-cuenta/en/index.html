🎣 Delete your Capturia account

Capturia · developed by Capturia Spain

Español English Français Italiano Português Deutsch العربية

From the app (immediate)

  1. Open Capturia and sign in.
  2. Tap your profile photo (top right) and go to My profile.
  3. Scroll to the bottom and tap Delete my account.
  4. Confirm by typing the word the app asks for.

Your account and its data are deleted at that moment and you are signed out.

Without the app (by email)

If you no longer have the app installed, write to us at capturiaSpain@gmail.com from the email address you registered with, with the subject "Delete my Capturia account". We will delete it within 30 days at most and confirm by email once it is done.

Delete only some data (without deleting your account)

In the app you can delete any catch, spot, own species, outing, big bite, photo, video, note, meetup, piece of gear, bait or link at any time: open it and tap Delete. It is deleted from your phone and from the cloud. You can also change or remove your profile photo from My profile.

If you prefer us to do it, email capturiaSpain@gmail.com from your account email saying which data you want deleted. We will do it within 30 days at most.

What is deleted

  • Your login account (email, Google or Apple sign-in) and your profile (username, photo, settings, links).
  • Your catches, spots, own species, blank days and big bites, with all their photos and videos stored in the cloud.
  • Your notes, meetups, group messages, gear (rods, reels, lines and other), baits and lures, links and your «What fish is it?» queries.
  • Your companions list, pending sends and the groups you have created.
  • Your plan and subscription history within Capturia.

What is not deleted

  • What other users have already accepted from you (catches, spots or species you sent them and they saved): it becomes theirs and stays in their account, without your name.
  • Google Play or App Store purchase receipts: the store keeps them according to its own policy.
  • The anonymous copy Capturia keeps without photos of your catches set to "Public (map)" or "Buddies and public" for the community map (species, weight, location, date and time): it is no longer linked to your account.
  • Technical server backups, if any, may keep your data for up to 30 days until they are renewed. The anonymous copy for the map is kept indefinitely.

Deleting your account does not cancel an active subscription on Google Play or the App Store. Cancel it first in the store (Google Play → Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions) so it does not renew.
