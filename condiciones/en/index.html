These terms apply to purchases made inside Capturia (the fishing app for Android/iOS): the Plus and Premium subscriptions, monthly or yearly, and the former one-time Plus for those who already bought it. By purchasing you accept these terms, as well as those of the store (Google Play or App Store) you pay through.

These terms are available in several languages. In case of discrepancy between versions, the Spanish version prevails.

1. Who you are buying from

Capturia is developed by Rubén Álvarez Felgueroso, an angler from Asturias, in northern Spain. A fishing lover, just like you, who wants you to have an app in your hand that helps you organise your catches and get all the information out of each one as simply as possible.

I also want you to be able to plan your next fishing day without jumping between different websites to check the weather and sea forecasts. And many other things… so many that I want to give you one month free to try the app (I recommend trying Premium).

Above all, I want you to know that Capturia will grow thanks to the suggestions of everyone who gets in touch at soporte@capturia.es. Don't hesitate to suggest any idea or correction, especially in the translations.

Capturia started as my personal project (then called FishApp) for my second higher vocational diploma in Application Development, and thanks to the interest and support of my fishing friends I decided to build an app with lots of features without raising the price much.

As for payment, it is always taken by the store you buy from (Google Play or App Store), using the payment method on your store account. Capturia never receives or stores your card details.

2. Plans and prices

  • Free: free of charge.
  • Plus: auto-renewing subscription, monthly or yearly.
  • Premium: auto-renewing subscription, monthly or yearly. You can subscribe to it directly, whether or not you have Plus. If you have Plus as a subscription and move to Premium, the store replaces one subscription with the other and credits the part of Plus you had already paid for and not used.
  • Legend: status that Capturia grants to people who help test the app. It includes the same as Premium, cannot be bought and involves no payment.

The price you pay is the one shown by the store at the time of purchase, in your currency and including your country's taxes (for example, VAT). The app's Subscription screen shows the monthly and yearly price of each plan, how much you save by paying yearly and what each plan includes.

Reference prices in Spain (VAT included): Plus €1.49/month or €11.99/year; Premium €1.99/month or €15.99/year. In other countries the price may vary depending on the currency and local taxes; if there is any difference, the price shown by the store at the time of purchase prevails.

3. Free trials

3.1 Free Plus trial. If you have never had any subscription in Capturia (neither Plus nor Premium), you can try it free for 1 month when you subscribe to the monthly plan. The trial is managed by the store: it will ask for a payment method and, when it ends, the subscription becomes paid and is charged automatically, unless you cancel it before it ends. If you cancel during the trial you are not charged anything and, when it ends, you go back to the Free plan.

The Plus trial can only be used once per Capturia account, per store account and per device. To prevent abuse, an encrypted device identifier is stored when it starts (see the privacy policy). Anyone who started Capturia's former Plus trial (without a payment method) keeps it until it ends, under the conditions it was activated with.

3.2 Free Premium trial. If you have never had any subscription in Capturia (neither Plus nor Premium), you can try Premium free for 1 month when you subscribe to the monthly plan. It is also managed by the store: when it ends you are charged automatically, unless you cancel it before. If you cancel it, you go back to your previous plan (Free, or Plus if you had the old one-time Plus). Only one free trial can be enjoyed: either the Plus one or the Premium one, not both.

3.3 Yearly plans do not include a trial period. Capturia may withdraw or change the trials in the future, without affecting those already in progress.

4. Renewal and cancellation

The Plus and Premium subscriptions renew automatically at the end of each period (every month or every year, depending on the one you choose) and are charged at the start of each period, until you cancel them.

You can cancel them at any time from the store (Google Play → Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions; App Store → Settings → your name → Subscriptions) or from Subscription → Manage or cancel my subscription in the app. When you cancel, you keep your plan until the end of the period already paid and you are not charged again; you then move to the best plan you still have (for example, one-time Plus) or to Free. If you switch between monthly and yearly from the store, the change applies at the next renewal.

Deleting the app or your Capturia account does not cancel the subscription: you have to cancel it in the store.

5. Price changes

Capturia may review its prices, in particular periodically (for example, once a year), to reflect changes in the cost of the service (servers, storage, store fees), changes in taxes or inflation, or when significant features are added.

  • Plus and Premium subscriptions: if the price goes up, we will notify you at least 30 days in advance through the store and/or the app itself. The new price will apply from the first renewal after that period and never to a period you have already paid for. If you disagree, you can cancel the subscription before it applies, at no cost. Where the store requires you to expressly accept the new price and you do not, the subscription will end at the end of the current period.
  • One-time Plus (earlier purchases): if you already bought it, you will not be charged again.
  • Offers and discounts: promotional prices are temporary and do not entitle you to keep them after the promotion ends, unless the offer itself states otherwise.

Price reductions may be applied without prior notice.

6. One-time Plus (earlier purchases)

Until 15 October 2026, Plus was sold as a one-time payment. Those who bought it keep it with no further payments for as long as they have their Capturia account and the app remains in operation, and can subscribe to Premium whenever they like (when Premium is cancelled, they go back to Plus). The features and limits of each plan may evolve over time (for example, to add improvements); if the benefits of a paid plan were ever significantly reduced, we would let you know in advance.

7. Right of withdrawal and refunds

As a consumer in the European Union, you generally have 14 days to withdraw from a distance purchase. However, since this is digital content activated at the moment of purchase, by paying you expressly request that Plus or Premium be activated immediately and acknowledge that you therefore lose your right of withdrawal (Article 16(m) of Directive 2011/83/EU and Article 103.m of Spanish Royal Legislative Decree 1/2007).

Refunds are handled by the store according to its own policies (for example, Google Play allows requests in some cases within the first 48 hours). If you have any problem with a purchase, write to us and we will help you. This does not limit the rights the law grants you if the service does not work as described.

8. Purchases and your account

Your purchase is linked to your Capturia account and to your store account. If you change phone or reinstall the app, sign in with the same account and use Restore purchases on the Subscription screen.

Username of Free accounts. So that usernames aren’t held by accounts that aren’t used, if your account is still on the Free plan 2 months after you sign up and you have never paid for Plus or Premium (the free trial doesn’t count as a payment), your username is released so someone else can choose it. Your account and your data are not deleted: if you come back, you just need to choose a new username. This does not apply to Legend accounts.
