Frequently asked questions

Is it free?

Yes. The Free plan never expires and lets you try all the basics. If you need more, you can upgrade to Plus or Premium from within the app.

Does it work without signal?

Yes. You can log catches and check your data offline. When you're back online, everything syncs automatically.

Can anyone see my spots?

No. Your spots and catches are private. Only the buddies you choose to share them with can see them.

How do I delete my account?

From the app or from the account deletion page. Your cloud data is deleted.
