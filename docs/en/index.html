Frequently asked questions

Is it free?

Yes. The Free plan never expires and lets you try the basics: 5 spots, 5 species, 10 catches with 1 photo each, importing your catches from the gallery and 2 “What fish is it?” identifications a month. With Free your data is stored only on your phone. If it falls short, you can move to Plus or Premium from the app itself.

What's the difference between Free, Plus and Premium?

Free is for trying the app. Plus is for people who fish often: 250 spots, 500 catches, 3 photos per catch, fishing forecast, fishing day weather and tides 4 days ahead, 500 MB cloud backup, groups and receiving what your buddies send you. Premium removes every limit: unlimited everything, 8 photos and video per catch, an 8-day forecast that learns from your history, condition alerts, advanced statistics, the public catches map, sending to buddies, creating groups and meetups, and 2 GB in the cloud.

Is there a free trial? How do I cancel?

The monthly Plus and Premium plans come with 1 month free if you've never had a Capturia subscription (one trial per account). Payment and cancellation go through your Google Play or App Store account: in the app, open Subscription and tap Manage. If you cancel, your plan stays active until the end of the period you've paid for, and nothing on your phone is deleted. If you cancel during the trial, you aren't charged.

Can I upgrade from Plus to Premium, or start straight on Premium?

Both. You don't need to go through Plus. If you already have Plus and switch to Premium, your subscription is changed and the store credits you for the unused part of Plus.

How do I import the catches I already have on my phone?

Open “Import my catches” from the menu or the home screen and pick the photos (on Android you can also choose a whole folder). You can also share photos to Capturia straight from your gallery. Capturia detects which photos have fish in them, groups photos taken at almost the same time into one catch and creates each catch with its date and time, ready for you to add the species, the spot and the weight. Photos you've already imported come up unticked so you don't duplicate them. It's available on every plan, but on Free it counts towards the 10-catch limit.

How do I share my catches with my buddies?

There are two ways. Send: open the catch, tap the send icon and choose one, several or all of your buddies; it reaches them with its photos and all the weather and sea data. Sending is a Premium feature; with Plus you can receive what others send you. Visibility: if you set a catch to “Buddies”, everyone on your list can see it without you sending it, and you choose which details they see besides the photo, the species and the weight. Catches you receive can't be edited or forwarded: they still belong to your buddy. You can also share a catch in a group chat.

What about WhatsApp, Instagram or Facebook?

Yes, on any plan. You can share the photo as it is, a card with the species, weight, length and date, or the same card in portrait for stories and statuses. The spot only appears if you tick it, and the images go out without the photo's GPS location. They carry a small Capturia mark and a QR code to download the app; with Premium you can remove them.

What statistics do I get?

Advanced statistics are a Premium feature. In “My catches” you see how many catches you have, at how many spots, the average and the biggest weight; the conditions that repeat most when you catch fish (time, moon, tide, wind…); your catches by month and by time of day (morning, afternoon and night); the weather and sea conditions of your catches (wind, rain, cloud, moon phase, sea state, swell, wave period, tide, water clarity and colour) and your results by type of spot and lure. You can filter by species, by period and by an area of the map. In “All users” you see the same with the public catches of every angler within 10 to 100 km, anonymously. The more details you log in your catches, the more accurate they are.

What's used from my catches if I make them public? Can people find out where I fish?

Everything is private by default: a catch is only public if you choose so. From a public catch, only the species, weight, place, date and time are used; never your name or your photos. On the map they're grouped into areas of at least 10 km, and an area only appears if it has catches from at least 2 different anglers, so nobody can see who caught what or the exact spot. Capturia keeps that anonymous copy even if you later delete the catch or make it private again. The map is a Premium feature and, to see it, you need to have contributed at least 3 public catches.

How does “What fish is it?” work?

Take a photo of the fish (or choose one from the gallery) and a specialised AI gives you up to 3 possible species with their % confidence, your photo next to the one from the official catalogue, the features it looks at and the approximate minimum size in your area. You get 2 identifications a month with Free, 10 with Plus and 50 with Premium; if one fails because of an error, it isn't counted. The photo is sent reduced, with no hidden data, and isn't stored. It needs a connection. The minimum size is only a guide: always check the regulations in your area.

What is the fishing forecast?

A score from 0 to 100 for every hour at your spots, combining the moon, the sun, pressure changes and, at sea, the tides and the swell. It highlights the best days and the best times of each day, with high and low tides at their exact time. With Plus you get 4 days; with Premium, 8 days, and it also learns from the times you catch the most. It's a guide, like in any fishing app: it doesn't guarantee catches, but it helps you choose when to go.

What are condition alerts?

You tell Capturia what sea, weather and tide you're looking for at one of your spots (for example, waves under 1 m, no rain and a rising tide, in the morning) and you get a notification when the forecast matches, even with the app closed. It can also alert you before a sharp pressure change or after a storm or several days of rain. They're a Premium feature, with up to 3 active alerts at a time.

Does it work with no signal?

Yes. You can log catches and check your data offline. When you're back in coverage, it syncs by itself. What needs internet is anything that relies on outside data, like “What fish is it?” or updating the forecast.

If I change phones, do I lose my data?

With Plus or Premium, no: you back up to the cloud (or turn on automatic upload) and on the new phone you sign in with your account and tap Restore data. Your subscription is recovered with Restore purchases, using the same store account. With Free your data is only on your phone, so it's lost if you change it. Documents in “My documents” never leave the phone, on any plan.

Can anyone see my spots?

No. Your spots and catches are private by default. Only the buddies you choose to share them with can see them, and if you make a catch public, it only counts on the map anonymously and by area.

How do I delete my account?

From the app or from the account deletion page. Your data is deleted from the cloud.
